## 1. App Positioning

QuestDeck is a **pocket deck of real-world activity cards** for people who are bored, stuck, or just looking for something to actually do.

It is not a productivity app, habit tracker, therapy tool, fitness app, social network, or AI assistant.

The closest analogy: a physical deck of activity cards you'd keep in a drawer — except your phone always has it with you.

**Target moments:**
- Sunday afternoon with nothing planned
- Sitting at home not knowing what to do
- Couple looking for a low-effort activity idea
- Friends together with no plan
- Person who wants to do something, not scroll

**Core promises:**
- Works offline
- No account, no login, no tracking
- Real activities, not advice
- Playful, not preachy

## 4. Full Description

*(For Google Play "Full description" field — up to 4000 characters. Plain text, no markdown.)*

---

Bored. Stuck. Not sure what to do with the next hour?

QuestDeck gives you a random deck of real-world activity cards to flip through and complete. Pick a mood, draw three cards, choose your quest, and go do something real.

No algorithm. No feed. No account. Just a simple card deck that works offline.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Pick a mood — bored, at home, outside, with a partner, with friends, weekend, creative, or need a reset
2. Three quest cards appear face-down. Tap to flip them one by one
3. Choose one quest and read the details: what to do, how long it takes, and an optional tip
4. Go do it. Mark it done when you finish
5. Earn XP and level up as you complete more quests

WHAT KIND OF QUESTS?

Short, doable, real-life activities. Some examples:

→ Go outside for 15 minutes and find something red, something blue, and something yellow
→ Sit near a window for 10 minutes with no phone. Notice five things outside
→ Make a drink and ask your partner two questions you have never asked before
→ Pick a street nearby you have never walked down and walk the full length
→ Write a complete story in exactly six words
→ Set a timer for 10 minutes and clean as much as you can before it goes off
→ Find a spot outside you have never sat before and stay for 20 minutes

Over 100 quests included — solo, couples, friends, and groups. Easy, medium, and hard difficulty. Something for every mood.

COMPLETELY PRIVATE

QuestDeck works entirely offline. It stores nothing in the cloud. It never asks for your name, email, or location. No account to create. No login. No tracking of any kind.

Your completed quests and XP live on your device only.

A SIMPLE XP SYSTEM

Earn XP for every quest you finish. Level up over time. See your history and streaks on the Progress screen. There are no leaderboards, no social feeds, and no notifications asking you to come back.

NOT THIS

QuestDeck is not a productivity tool, habit tracker, self-improvement guide, or AI assistant. It is a simple, playful activity deck for people who want to spend less time staring at a screen and more time actually doing something.

Pick a mood. Flip the cards. Go.

## 7. App Icon Concept

*(512 × 512 px for Google Play, must also generate adaptive icon)*

**Background:** solid warm orange `#FF8C42`

**Foreground mark:**
- Three white playing-card shapes, stacked slightly fanned (like a hand of cards being held), viewed from slightly above
- Cards are simple white rectangles with rounded corners, no detailed patterns — clean and bold
- A small five-pointed star ✦ on the top card's face (echoes the XP/quest concept)
- Mark fills roughly 65% of the canvas with comfortable padding

**Do not include:**
- The word "Quest" or "QD" as text inside the icon — marks, not labels
- Gradients on the cards — flat white reads better at small sizes
- More than 3 cards — visual clutter at 48px

**Why this works:** The card fan is universally recognizable as "game / deck." The orange is warm and distinctive on the dark Play Store background. The icon reads clearly at 48px (notification bar size).

## 10. Claims and Phrases to Avoid

**Superlatives (policy violation risk):**
- "best", "#1", "top", "most popular", "highest rated"
- "world's first", "only app that"

**Promotional phrasing (policy risk or just bad tone):**
- "free" as a feature claim
- "download now"
- "limited time"
- "join millions"

**Medical / mental health (policy violation):**
- "relieves anxiety"
- "reduces stress"
- "mental health"
- "therapy"
- "mindfulness" (unless it's literally a mindfulness app)
- "wellbeing" (in a medical framing)

**Fitness / health (wrong positioning):**
- "fitness"
- "workout"
- "health goals"
- "burn calories"

**Productivity (wrong positioning):**
- "boost productivity"
- "be more productive"
- "achieve your goals"
- "self-improvement"
- "habit building"
- "life coach"

**Tech claims (misleading):**
- "AI-powered"
- "powered by AI"
- "machine learning"
- "smart suggestions"

**Store ranking / awards (policy violation):**
- "Editor's Choice"
- "Featured on Play Store"
- "Award-winning"
- Any implied Google endorsement
