B — Stack-only (immersive flow)

Home screen is the entire app. Progress and Packs are icon buttons top-right — accessible but not competing for attention. Quest flow goes forward screen by screen, back button returns. Feels like a card deck, not a dashboard.

Pros

  • Playful, not utilitarian
  • Focus stays on the quest flow
  • Less chrome = bigger cards

Cons

  • Progress/Packs slightly less discoverable
